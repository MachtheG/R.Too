## Context for the draft promises (farmers and women)
- **Tea:** This is Bomet's top value chain. Farmers are disputing the green-leaf rate (KSh 23 against a promised KSh 26).
- **Dairy:** Bomet has a milk shortage driven by feed costs. Sotik's listed constraints are cooling plants, improved breeds and disease control.
- **County policy:** The county is focusing on value addition for farm produce.

## Gaps: what to get from the campaign
1. **Biography:** full name, education, career, community and church roles, family (optional), and honours, each with a supporting document.
2. **The "Hon." title:** the nomination body, year and gazette notice. Until then the site says "Hon. Robert Too" with no office stated.
3. **Track record:** the projects, groups or initiatives he has led, each with a document or photo.
4. **Photos:** links to his official pages. Search found no verified accounts, and several unrelated Kenyan "Robert Too" profiles exist, so **I won't guess which one is him**.
5. **Contacts:** the WhatsApp channel link, an email address, and the "authorised by" agent's name.
