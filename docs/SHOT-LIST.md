# Shot list: photos needed

Drop each file into `public/images/<category>/`, run `npm run images`, then fill its slot in `data/photos.ts` (`file`, alt and caption in EN + SW, date, ward, credit, licence, approvedForPress). Natural light and documentary style: Robert Too with people, never alone on a stage. **No AI-generated images of any real person.**

| Slot id | Where it appears | Ratio | Min size | The shot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `hero-portrait` | Home hero (full-bleed) | 4:5 | 1600×2000 | Waist-up portrait among tea farmers on a terrace, eye-level, morning light |
| `story-home` | Story page lead | 4:5 | 1600×2000 | At home or on his farm in Sotik, relaxed, looking off-camera |
| `story-family` | Story page | 3:2 | 1800×1200 | With family or community elders, seated conversation |
| `tea-buying-centre` | Plan pledge 1, Gallery | 3:2 | 1800×1200 | Listening to growers at a tea buying centre, scales/leaf visible |

| `town-hall` | Gallery, News | 16:9 | 1920×1080 | Addressing a ward meeting; show the crowd, not just the podium |
| `sotik-hills` | Gallery, textures | 16:9 | 2400×1350 | Landscape of tea terraces, no identifiable people |

| Record teaser | Home | 16:9 | 1920×1080 | At a community project site he supported (needs a matching document) |

For every photo, record the **photographer or account (credit)**, the **licence or permission**, and **the date and place**. Press photos need the outlet's permission and credit.
